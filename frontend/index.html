About the Night

Amor Regius is an evening dedicated to elegance, gratitude, and excellence.

Hosted by the RoyalHouse Chapel Students Association at the University of Ghana, this Dinner & Awards Night brings the community together for fine dining, live entertainment, and the recognition of members, departments, and executives whose contributions made the year memorable.

Whether you're joining to celebrate with friends, support an award nominee, or simply enjoy a night of style and substance, Amor Regius is designed to be an experience worth dressing up for.

How Payment Works

Both ticket purchases and votes are paid via MTN Mobile Money, right on this site:

  1. Choose your ticket (or nominee) and enter your MTN Mobile Money number.
  2. Tap Pay — a payment prompt is sent straight to your phone.
  3. Approve it with your Mobile Money PIN.
  4. Your order confirms automatically and your ticket is emailed to you — no codes to copy.

We never ask for or store your Mobile Money PIN. You approve every payment directly on your own phone.
